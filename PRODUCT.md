# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Dubai residents, tourists, and families browsing on mobile or desktop. They are price-conscious, not luxury-seeking. They want to quickly understand what cars are available, confirm it fits their budget, and contact the business by WhatsApp. They are often in a "ready to book now" mindset: they've already decided they need a rental, they just need to trust this specific company.

## Product Purpose

Royal Rides Car Rental is an affordable car rental agency in Dubai. The website's sole job is to convert visitors into WhatsApp inquiries and booking requests. Success is measured by taps on "WhatsApp Now" and "Request Availability." Fleet browsing and plan comparison are supporting steps on that path.

## Brand Personality

Reliable, clear, approachable. Not flashy, not corporate, not luxury. The brand feels like a trusted local business that's easy to deal with and straightforward about pricing.

Three words: honest, bright, practical.

## Anti-references

- Ultra-luxury car rental sites (Lamborghini, Exotic car rental Dubai vibes): dark backgrounds, cinematic hero videos, gold accents, dramatic typography.
- Generic SaaS landing pages: metric hero blocks, identical feature card grids, glassmorphism panels.
- Budget-airline cheapness: cluttered layouts, aggressive discount badges, low-quality stock photos.
- Corporate coldness: navy + white + grey with zero personality.

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity earns trust.** Every element should make the next step obvious. If a visitor has to search for the CTA, the design failed.
2. **Warmth without whimsy.** The brand is bright and approachable, not playful or cute. Keep it professional but human.
3. **Practical over decorative.** Every visual element should serve a real communication job. Decoration for its own sake adds noise.
4. **Mobile is the primary surface.** Dubai tourists and residents book on their phones. Every layout decision should work on mobile first.
5. **Speed of trust.** A visitor should be able to feel "this is a real, reliable business" within the first 5 seconds of the page loading.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Arabic-speaking users are part of the audience; layouts should not assume LTR-only reading. Reduced motion support required for scroll animations.
