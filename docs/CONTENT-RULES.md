# Content Rules

## Core Messaging

Royal Rides Car Rental Dubai offers sedan, SUV, and budget car rentals with doorstep delivery, airport transfers, transparent pricing, flexible rental plans, and 24/7 support.

The website must make booking feel:
Simple
Fast
Trustworthy
Clear
WhatsApp friendly

## Voice And Tone

Use a tone that is:
Clear
Friendly
Direct
Trustworthy
Simple
Helpful
Modern
Conversion focused

Avoid tone that is:
Overly luxury
Hype heavy
Corporate cold
Generic
Confusing
Aggressive

## Do Not Use

Do not use:
Lorem ipsum
Generic placeholder copy
Fake review counts
Fake Google rating claims
Fake awards
Fake inventory status
Fake prices
Fake discounts
Unverified claims
Overused lines like “your trusted partner”

## Copy Priorities

Every section should answer one of these:
What do they offer?
Why should I trust them?
How easy is it to book?
What type of car can I get?
Can they deliver to me?
Can I book through WhatsApp?
Can I rent daily, weekly, or monthly?
What do I do next?

## Required Phrases And Concepts

Use these concepts naturally:
Affordable car rental in Dubai
Sedan cars
SUV cars
Budget cars
Doorstep delivery
Airport transfers
Transparent pricing
Flexible rental plans
Daily rentals
Weekly rentals
Monthly rentals
24/7 support
WhatsApp booking
Request availability

Good headline options:

CTA labels:
WhatsApp Now
Browse Cars
Request Availability

## Category Copy Guidance

Sedan Cars:
Comfortable daily rentals for city drives, business trips, and simple point to point travel in Dubai.

SUV Cars:
Spacious rental options for families, groups, airport pickups, and longer drives around the UAE.

Budget Cars:
Affordable cars for everyday use, short trips, weekly rentals, and monthly plans.

## Featured Car Copy Rules

If exact inventory is missing:
Use example cards.
Do not claim live availability.
Use language like:
Popular sedan option
Budget friendly choice
Family friendly SUV
Ask for current availability

## Review Copy Rules

Use real reviews only when provided.
If real reviews are not provided, use general testimonial style copy without platform ratings.

Acceptable:
“Booking was simple and the car was delivered on time.”

Not acceptable:
“Rated 4.9 stars by 2,000 customers” unless true and provided.

## FAQ Copy Rules

Required FAQ:
Do you offer doorstep delivery in Dubai?
Can I book through WhatsApp?
Do you offer airport transfers?
Can I rent daily, weekly, or monthly?
What car types are available?
Are prices transparent?
Do you provide 24/7 support?

Answers must be short, clear, and specific.
