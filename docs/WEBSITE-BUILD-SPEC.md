# Website Build Spec

## Business

Business Name: Royal Rides Car Rental  
Website Type: Affordable car rental agency website  
Location / Service Area: Dubai, UAE  

## Website Goal

The website must generate:
WhatsApp inquiries
Booking requests
Fleet browsing
Request availability actions

Primary user journey:
Visitor lands on homepage.
Visitor understands Royal Rides offers affordable car rentals in Dubai.
Visitor views sedan, SUV, and budget categories.
Visitor checks featured cars or plans.
Visitor clicks WhatsApp or request availability.

## Brand Positioning

Royal Rides Car Rental should feel:
Clean
Affordable
Trustworthy
Family friendly
Modern
Easy to book
Reliable
Bright
Practical

The website should not feel:
Ultra luxury
Dark
Cinematic exotic
Aggressive
Overpriced
Generic
Corporate cold

## Core Message

Affordable car rental in Dubai with sedan, SUV, and budget cars, doorstep delivery, airport transfers, transparent pricing, flexible rental plans, and 24/7 support.

## Audience

Dubai residents who need short or long term rentals.
Tourists who need simple car rental.
Families who need practical cars.
Customers who want clear pricing.
Customers who want WhatsApp booking.
Customers who want airport or doorstep delivery.

## Services And Offers

Sedan cars
SUV cars
Budget cars
Doorstep delivery
Airport transfers
Daily rentals
Weekly rentals
Monthly rentals
24/7 support

## Homepage Sitemap

Use this exact order:

1. Header
2. Hero Section
3. Quick Booking Search
4. Trust Bar
5. Car Categories
6. Featured Cars
7. Doorstep Delivery and Airport Transfers
8. Simple Rental Process
9. Popular Rental Plans
10. Reviews
11. FAQ
12. Final CTA
13. Footer

## Section Details

### Header

Include:
Royal Rides logo or text logo
Navigation links
WhatsApp CTA
View Cars CTA if useful
Mobile menu

Goal:
Give users fast access to the booking path.

### Hero Section

Suggested headline:
Affordable Car Rental in Dubai

Suggested subheadline:
Rent sedan, SUV, and budget cars with doorstep delivery, airport transfers, flexible plans, and 24/7 support.

CTAs:
WhatsApp Now
Browse Cars

Trust points:
Daily, weekly and monthly rentals
Doorstep delivery across Dubai
Airport transfers available
Transparent pricing

### Quick Booking Search

Fields:
Pick up location
Rental date
Car type
Rental plan

CTA:
Request Availability

Behavior:
Should support front end only inquiry flow.
Can direct to WhatsApp.
Do not add backend unless explicitly requested.

### Trust Bar

Trust points:
Doorstep delivery
Airport transfers
24/7 support
Transparent pricing
Flexible plans

### Car Categories

Cards:
Sedan Cars
SUV Cars
Budget Cars

Each card:
Title
Short description
View Category CTA
Icon or image slot

### Featured Cars

Show car cards.
If exact inventory is missing, use example based car cards without claiming live availability.

Each card:
Car name or example label
Category
Features
CTA: Check Availability

Do not invent live stock.
Do not invent exact pricing unless provided.

### Doorstep Delivery And Airport Transfers

Mention:
Doorstep delivery across Dubai
Airport pickup
Airport transfer support
Hotel delivery
Easy handover
WhatsApp coordination

### Simple Rental Process

Steps:
1. Choose your car
2. Send a WhatsApp inquiry
3. Confirm rental details
4. Get delivery or pickup

### Popular Rental Plans

Plans:
Daily Rentals
Weekly Rentals
Monthly Rentals

Each plan:
Who it is best for
Short benefit list
WhatsApp CTA

### Reviews

Use real reviews only if provided.
If real reviews are not provided, write general testimonial style copy without fake platform claims or fake review counts.

### FAQ

Required FAQ:
Do you offer doorstep delivery in Dubai?
Can I book through WhatsApp?
Do you offer airport transfers?
Can I rent daily, weekly, or monthly?
What car types are available?
Are prices transparent?
Do you provide 24/7 support?

### Final CTA

Purpose:
Push user to WhatsApp or request availability.

Tone:
Simple
Friendly
Clear
No pressure

### Footer

Include:
Royal Rides Car Rental
Dubai, UAE
Quick links
Car categories
Services
WhatsApp CTA
Basic legal links where useful
