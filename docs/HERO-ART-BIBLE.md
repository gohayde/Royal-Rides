# Royal Rides Hero Art Bible

Use this as the visual source of truth for extending the homepage after the completed hero. The goal is to keep every future section aligned with the same clean, bright, practical rental-car feel.

## Palette

- Primary red: `#DA5259`
- Active/deeper red: `#DF3F4D`
- Primary ink: `#0C1324`
- Body/muted text: `#5E6573`
- Soft border: `#DDE0E6`
- Main background: `#F8F8F6`
- Surface white: `#FFFFFF`

Rules:

- Use red for CTAs, active nav states, key icons, selected states, and small brand accents.
- Use ink for headings, strong labels, and navigation.
- Use muted text for body copy and form values.
- Keep backgrounds white or warm off-white. Do not introduce dark luxury sections.
- Red should feel friendly and useful, not aggressive. Avoid neon, black gradients, and exotic-car color treatment.

## Typography Rules

- Headings use `Plus Jakarta Sans`, bold or extra-bold.
- Body, navigation, buttons, forms, labels, and supporting text use `Inter`.
- Hero headline style is uppercase, extra-bold, tight but readable tracking, and strong line breaks.
- Future section headings should stay bold and clean, but not as oversized as the hero.
- Body copy should be medium weight, generous line height, and easy to scan.
- Avoid tiny captions. Secondary text should remain readable on mobile.

Recommended hierarchy:

- Hero H1: very large uppercase display, 3-line structure.
- Section H2: bold, clean, 44-64px desktop, 32-42px tablet, 28-34px mobile.
- Body copy: 18-22px desktop, 16-18px mobile.
- Labels/nav: 16-19px, semibold.
- Button text: 16-18px, bold.

## Spacing Rules

- Use generous horizontal gutters: about 5vw on desktop, 24px tablet, 16px mobile.
- Keep large vertical breathing room between major sections.
- Align content to strong invisible columns, not centered generic blocks everywhere.
- Hero spacing is wide and airy: nav floats at the top, text starts below with confident whitespace, booking panel overlaps the lower visual field.
- Cards and forms should have internal padding large enough to feel touch-friendly.
- Avoid crowded grids. Three-column areas should have clear gaps and enough white space.

## Layout Principles

- Use a bright, open first-impression layout.
- Keep one strong focal image or visual idea per section.
- Favor left-aligned copy with a practical visual or card system on the right.
- Maintain the hero's balance: strong text block, soft white surface, red accent area, and car imagery as the product signal.
- Future sections should feel like they belong below this hero, not like separate templates.
- Use rounded floating surfaces sparingly for important modules such as search, plans, and feature cards.
- Do not overbuild a large design system before sections exist. Reuse simple, specific components.

## Image Treatment

- Use clean, bright vehicle imagery or image-led cards.
- Vehicle images should sit on white or soft neutral space with natural shadows.
- Avoid dark cinematic car photography, exotic supercar crops, and high-contrast luxury lighting.
- Preserve natural car proportions. Do not overcrop cars into abstract shapes.
- Red decorative fields may sit behind cars, but should be broad, simple, and rounded/curved like the hero.
- Use subtle overlays only for readability. Do not wash the vehicle image with heavy color overlays.
- Background patterns should stay soft: light dots, soft vignettes, faint shadows.

## Motion Rules

- Motion should be subtle, premium, and useful.
- Use gentle entrance reveals for major sections, cards, and CTAs.
- Keep hover motion small: 1-2px lift, soft shadow increase, slight color emphasis.
- Do not animate every element.
- Avoid dealership-style effects, spinning cars, flashing badges, or aggressive scroll effects.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.

Suggested motion:

- Header and hero content: fade/slide in lightly.
- Cards: staggered fade-up.
- Buttons: small lift and stronger shadow on hover.
- Icons: no looping animation unless purposeful.

## Button Style

- Primary buttons use red `#DA5259` or a very subtle red gradient.
- Button text is white, bold, and direct.
- Radius should be rounded but not pill-only: around 14-16px for normal CTAs.
- Large search/booking buttons can be square-rounded with strong icon scale.
- Primary hover: small upward lift, slightly deeper shadow, no dramatic color shift.
- Secondary buttons use white surface, soft gray border, ink text, and red-accent hover border.
- Focus states must be visible with a soft red outline.

## Nav Style

- Navigation should feel like the hero: floating white rounded container, soft shadow, generous spacing.
- Brand mark is red, wordmark is dark and bold.
- Nav links are simple text, no heavy boxes.
- Active nav link uses red.
- Right side keeps a secondary call button and primary booking button.
- Desktop nav should stay airy; do not cram extra items.
- Mobile nav should simplify quickly. Keep logo and primary booking action visible.

## Mobile Adaptation Rules

- Preserve the visual mood, but prioritize readability over exact desktop composition.
- Keep the primary CTA visible in the header.
- Allow the car/background image to become atmospheric behind the content, with stronger white gradient overlays.
- Headline should remain bold and large, but must not overflow.
- If a long headline line breaks, prefer clean two-word or phrase-based breaks.
- Booking/search form stacks vertically as a white rounded card.
- Form rows need clear labels, large touch targets, and visible dividers.
- Avoid horizontal scrolling.
- Do not hide conversion actions on mobile.

## Future Section Feel

Future homepage sections should feel:

- Bright, trustworthy, affordable, and polished.
- Practical for families, tourists, and Dubai residents.
- Conversion-focused without looking pushy.
- Built around clear browsing and WhatsApp booking.
- More like a clean rental service than a luxury supercar brand.

Section guidance:

- Quick Booking Search: extend the hero booking style with white rounded surfaces, red icons, and clear fields.
- Trust Bar: compact white or transparent cards with red line icons and concise trust copy.
- Car Categories: three clean cards for Sedan, SUV, Budget; use bright vehicle imagery or icon slots.
- Featured Cars: practical fleet cards with white surfaces, soft borders, clear category labels, and red availability CTAs.
- Delivery/Airport Transfers: split layout with strong copy on one side and a soft visual/service card on the other.
- Rental Process: simple numbered steps, red icons, minimal copy, generous gaps.
- Rental Plans: three cards with daily, weekly, monthly emphasis; no invented prices unless provided.
- Reviews: light testimonial cards, friendly copy, no fake platform claims.
- FAQ: clean accordion/list styling with strong question text and muted concise answers.
- Final CTA: echo the hero palette and rounded surfaces, with a clear WhatsApp action.
- Footer: light, clean, not dark-heavy.

## Do Not Do

- Do not turn the site into a black luxury/exotic car brand.
- Do not add generic SaaS-style gradients or random decorative blobs.
- Do not invent heavy animations or dealership gimmicks.
- Do not use fake review counts, fake live availability, or fake exact pricing.
- Do not overuse cards until the page feels boxed-in.
- Do not change the hero asset treatment unless a direct visual fidelity issue requires it.
